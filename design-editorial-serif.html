Wireless Information and Intelligence Laboratory (WIIL) is a joint research group at HUFS, co-directed by Prof. Jung Hoon Lee (이정훈) and Prof. Kyungrak Son (손경락), working on next-generation wireless communications, coding and information theory, signal processing, and machine learning for the intelligent edge.

Research Highlights

  • LEO Satellite Communications — beam-squint mitigation, tensor-based channel estimation, random-beam NOMA
  • Coding & Information Theory — index coding, coded computing, straggler mitigation via Steiner systems
  • Signal Processing & ML — ML-based beamforming/power allocation, ISAC, ML-aided communication
  • Physical-Layer Security — jammer/relay selection, covert communications, secrecy DoF
