🔬 About the Lab

Wireless Information and Intelligence Laboratory (WIIL) is a joint research group at HUFS, co-directed by Prof. Jung Hoon Lee (이정훈) and Prof. Kyungrak Son (손경락). We study the theory and systems behind next-generation wireless communications and information processing — spanning wireless communications, coding & information theory, signal processing, and machine learning. Our flagship thrusts include LEO satellite communications, coded/distributed computing for edge AI, and physical-layer security.

🧩 Research Highlights

  • LEO Satellite Communications — beam-squint mitigation, tensor-based & decentralized channel estimation, random-beam NOMA
  • Coding & Information Theory — index coding, coded computing, coded matrix computation, straggler mitigation via Steiner systems
  • Signal Processing & Machine Learning — ML-based beamforming/power allocation, ISAC, machine-learning-aided communication
  • Physical-Layer Security — opportunistic jammer/relay selection, covert communications, secrecy degrees of freedom
